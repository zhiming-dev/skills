# Review checklist

1. Confirm the answer addresses the question and the evidence supports each consequential claim.
2. Check source links, observation dates, units, denominators, and treatment of missing or incomplete cases.
3. Reconcile calculations and charts with their source data. Verify a typical case and a consequential outlier where applicable.
4. Explain assumptions and meaningful limitations in plain language. Preserve useful charts through copy revisions.
5. For PDFs, render every page and verify fonts, labels, links, page numbers, and visual hierarchy. Look for overlaps, clipping, tiny text, and awkward breaks.
6. Remove unrelated private data and deliver the report in the requested format.
